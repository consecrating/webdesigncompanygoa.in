Service website clarity

Scenario: A business serves customers in Goa but its current website makes services difficult to compare.

Method: Map visitor questions, simplify the page hierarchy, define useful proof requirements and create a direct enquiry path.

Review focus: Content completeness, mobile reading order, descriptive links and clear next actions.
