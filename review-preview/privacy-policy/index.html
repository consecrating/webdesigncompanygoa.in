Last updated: 30 August 2026

About this website

Web Design Company Goa is a focused web-design website by Sanctify. The current site publishes information and links. It does not provide user accounts, a contact form, ecommerce checkout or an application backend.

Information collected by this site

The website does not intentionally collect personal information through forms. It does not include analytics, advertising trackers, marketing pixels or cookies set by site scripts.

Like most web hosting, the hosting environment may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, requested path, time, browser information and server logs. The exact hosting retention and operational controls depend on the hosting provider.

External Sanctify website

Project enquiries continue on Sanctify's external contact page. When you follow that link, you leave this website. Review Sanctify's privacy policy before providing personal information through its pages, forms or services.

Other external links

External websites control their own content, security and privacy practices. A link does not transfer their privacy practices to this site.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the website's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the current published version.
