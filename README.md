# webdesigncompanygoa.in

SEO research, two-domain canonical ownership, and implementation planning for a Goa-focused web-design specialist operated transparently by Sanctify. Sanctify remains the main business/marketing entity; the specialist owns distinct web-design intent.

## SEO deliverables

- [`docs/seo/two-domain-seo-strategy.md`](docs/seo/two-domain-seo-strategy.md) — domain roles, policy risks, canonical ownership, migration, redirects, entity/schema/GBP rules, linking, measurement, rollback, and success criteria.
- [`docs/seo/sanctify-web-design-migration-map.csv`](docs/seo/sanctify-web-design-migration-map.csv) — reviewed source-to-destination actions for Sanctify’s overlapping web-design URLs.

- [`docs/seo/keyword-strategy.md`](docs/seo/keyword-strategy.md) — research findings, priorities, page ownership, local SEO plan, and guardrails.
- [`docs/seo/business-facts-required.md`](docs/seo/business-facts-required.md) — approval gate for location, NAP, services, platforms, proof, claims, and commercial facts.
- [`docs/seo/keyword-map.csv`](docs/seo/keyword-map.csv) — 310 service, commercial, industry, and informational rows with explicit site owner and absolute canonical URL.
- [`docs/seo/local-seo-keywords.csv`](docs/seo/local-seo-keywords.csv) — 300 Goa, regional, town, proximity, and local-search rows split between Sanctify and the specialist.
- [`docs/seo/content-roadmap.csv`](docs/seo/content-roadmap.csv) — 29 phased build, improvement, and consolidation work items with canonical ownership.
- [`tools/build_keyword_inventory.py`](tools/build_keyword_inventory.py) — dependency-free generator for the three inventories plus ownership, canonical, roadmap, and migration-readiness validation.

## Rebuild the inventories

```bash
python3 tools/build_keyword_inventory.py
```

The research snapshot is dated **2026-08-30**. Search volume, keyword difficulty, and CPC fields are deliberately blank until populated from a dated Google Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner, or approved third-party export. No metrics or business claims have been fabricated.
