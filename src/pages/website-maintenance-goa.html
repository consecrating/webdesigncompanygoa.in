What ongoing care can include

A suitable plan depends on the platform, the content and how often the site changes.

Updates and patches

Core software, plugins and dependencies are kept current to reduce known risks.

Backups and recovery

Regular backups and a restore approach support recovery from mistakes or failures.

Content edits

Routine text, image and small layout changes are handled without a full rebuild.

Health checks

Links, forms, speed and basic accessibility are reviewed on a practical schedule.
