Discovery areas for an online store

Each area informs scope, platform selection and what needs confirmation before work begins.

Products and catalogue

Product types, categories, variants, media, stock information and content ownership.

Cart and checkout

Customer steps, payment requirements, tax context, policies and confirmation communication.

Fulfilment and service

Delivery rules, collection options, order handling, returns and customer support responsibilities.

Store operations

Editing roles, catalogue updates, reporting needs, maintenance and external system expectations.

Platform selection is conditional

No named platform or integration is presented as verified support on this website.

A recommendation should follow the catalogue model, checkout needs, operational workflow, hosting context, available budget and maintenance plan. Payment, shipping, inventory or business-system connections need separate feasibility confirmation.

Discovery should also identify policy content, product-data readiness, photography needs and who will keep the store accurate after launch.

Content is part of the store system

Useful product pages depend on accurate information, clear media and consistent decisions, not interface polish alone.

Navigation, search or filtering needs should follow the real catalogue. Customer policies and operational details need approval from the business responsible for them.

Ecommerce development questions

How is an ecommerce platform selected?

Platform selection follows discovery. Catalogue size, checkout needs, operational workflow, hosting, budget and maintenance responsibilities all influence the decision.

What should be prepared before an online store project?

Prepare product categories, product information, fulfilment rules, payment requirements, customer policies, content responsibilities and the people who will operate the store.

Are specific integrations included?

No integration is assumed. Any payment, shipping, inventory or business-system connection needs to be confirmed for feasibility and scope during discovery.
