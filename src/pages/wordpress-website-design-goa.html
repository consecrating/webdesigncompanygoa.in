WordPress suits many content-led websites, but the decision should follow the requirements rather than a fixed platform claim.

  • Editable contentA clear editing model lets the right people update pages without a developer for routine changes.
  • Responsive templatesTemplates are planned for readable, accessible layouts across screen sizes.

What a WordPress build considers

Theme and templates

WooCommerce, if needed

Feasibility comes before the platform

Hosting, editing frequency, required functionality and long-term maintenance all affect whether WordPress is the right fit for a specific project.

No plugin, theme or integration is assumed before requirements are verified. If a simpler approach meets the approved needs, it is preferred.

A useful WordPress brief covers: the content types, who edits the site, required features, current hosting and any systems the site must connect to.
