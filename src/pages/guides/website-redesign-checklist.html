Website Redesign Checklist

A checklist for Goa businesses planning a redesign, covering goals, content, mobile, speed, SEO migration and the safeguards that protect traffic during a rebuild.

Plan a redesignBefore you start

Prepare before anything is rebuilt

Good decisions come from evidence, so gather the facts before choosing a new direction.

  • Write down the goals the new site must achieve
  • List current URLs and your top performing pages
  • Note where enquiries and traffic come from today
  • Identify content that must be kept, improved or retired
  • Confirm who owns content and who approves decisions
  • Record known problems on mobile, speed and navigation
  • Gather brand assets, logos and any style direction
  • Agree a realistic timeline and budget range

Decisions to make during the rebuild

These choices shape both the experience and the result.

Structure and navigation

Reorganise pages around visitor questions so the most important paths are short and clear.

Mobile and speed

Rebuild layouts to stay fast and comfortable on phones, where most visitors arrive.

Content quality

Improve copy and media so pages answer real questions rather than repeating filler.

Clear actions

Give each key page one obvious next step so more visits become enquiries.

Protect SEO through the migration

The riskiest moment is launch, so migration is planned before the old site is replaced.

A URL map, planned redirects, preserved content and checks after launch protect SEO, so existing rankings and links are carried across to the new website. Watch indexing and traffic closely for a few weeks after go live, and keep the ability to fix redirects quickly if anything is missed.

Redesign checklist questions

What are the signs a website needs a redesign?

Signs include a dated look, poor mobile use, slow loading, content that is hard to update and pages that no longer guide visitors toward a clear action.

What should be prepared before a redesign?

Prepare current URLs, top performing pages, analytics context, content owners and clear goals, so decisions are grounded in evidence rather than personal taste alone.

How is SEO protected during a rebuild?

A URL map, planned redirects, preserved content and checks after launch protect SEO, so existing rankings and links are carried across to the new website.
