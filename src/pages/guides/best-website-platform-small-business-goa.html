There is no single winner

The right platform depends on your needs, not on a fixed favourite, so match the tool to the job.

  • Budget and timeSome platforms cost less to start, while others invest more upfront for flexibility.
  • Who edits the siteThe ease of everyday updates matters as much as the initial build.
  • Room to growConsider where the business is heading before locking into one approach.
Questions to answer first: what features you truly need, who will update the site, how much control you want and how you expect the business to change.

Common routes, honestly compared

Each option suits different situations rather than every business.

WordPress

Flexible and widely supported, with strong ownership, though it needs care and updates over time.

Hosted platforms

Simple to run and maintain, trading some control and flexibility for convenience and predictable costs.

Hosted stores

Built for selling, with checkout and payments handled, best when ecommerce is the main purpose.

Custom builds

Made for specific needs, offering full control at a higher cost that suits particular requirements.

Choose well now to avoid pain later

A platform can be changed later, but moving is real work, so a sensible early choice pays off.

Platform choice questions

Which website platform is best for a small business?

There is no single winner. The best platform depends on your budget, the features you need, who will edit the site and how much control and growth you expect.

Is WordPress or a hosted platform better?

WordPress offers flexibility and ownership, while a hosted platform trades some control for simplicity, so the right choice follows your workflow and long term plans.

Can the platform be changed later?

A platform can be changed later, but migration takes effort, so choosing a suitable option early and planning content sensibly reduces cost and disruption over time.
