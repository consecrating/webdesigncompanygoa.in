Direct bookings protect margin

Portals bring reach but take commission, so a strong direct channel keeps more of each booking.

  • Clear direct valueGuests need an obvious reason to book on your site rather than a portal.
  • Fast and simpleA quick mobile site with an easy booking step reduces drop off.
  • Trust at the moment of decisionHonest rates and reassurance help guests commit directly.
What supports direct booking: clear offers, honest rates, fast mobile pages, visible contact, trust signals and a booking or enquiry step that is easy to complete.

Why guests use portals, and how to respond

Understanding the habit is the first step to shifting it.

Perceived best price

Show your direct offers clearly so guests see the value of booking with the hotel.

Convenience

Make the direct booking or enquiry step at least as easy as the portal experience.

Trust and reviews

Provide honest content and reassurance so guests feel safe booking directly.

Habit

Give a clear, memorable reason to return to your site for the next stay.

Balance, not a war with portals

The aim is a healthier mix, not abandoning the reach that portals provide.

Portals still bring visibility, so use them for reach while steadily growing lower cost direct bookings through the hotel website. Over time, a clear direct offer and a fast, trustworthy site shift more of the booking mix toward the channel the hotel controls.

Direct booking questions

How can a hotel win more direct bookings?

Clear direct offers, honest rates, fast mobile pages, trust signals and a simple booking or enquiry path give guests a reason to book directly with the hotel.

Why do guests book through portals instead?

Guests use portals for habit, perceived deals and convenience, so a hotel site must make the direct value obvious and the booking step easy to overcome that pull.

Does direct booking mean leaving portals entirely?

No. Portals still bring visibility, and the aim is balance, using them for reach while steadily growing lower cost direct bookings through the hotel website.
